### Selected Experiments (rank by expected ROI)

#### [1]. Speculative Decoding Warmup & Stability (vLLM MRV2 DFlash/DSpark) — vLLM (main/latest)
**Hypothesis**: The JIT warmup gap for Speculative Decoding on Blackwell (MRV2) architectures has been closed. We expect a significant reduction in TTFT (Time to First Token) variance and improved cold-start latency for speculative workloads, transitioning from "working proofs" to stable production kernels. Without this fix, spec-decode suffers from massive initial latency penalties due to unoptimized JIT compilation during the first few requests.
**Setup**:
  - Model: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (standard for benchmarking, fits easily in 128GB)
  - Framework version: `vllm/vllm-flashattention:latest` (ensure commit includes `MRV2 spec-decode JIT fixes`)
  - Node config: Single-node (`spark-01`) to isolate kernel performance from inter-node network variance.
  - Parallelism: TP=1, PP=1, Speculative Decoding: `draft_model=Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`, `num_speculative_tokens=8`.
